About the School Managment

Our student website is designed to make learning simple, interactive, and organized. We aim to:
1.Provide easy access to academic information
2.Help students prepare for exams with online tests
3.Track performance and improve results
4.Encourage collaboration through discussion and feedback
Mission: To create a smart digital platform where every student can learn, test, and grow. Vision: Empower students with technology-driven education tools.

📚 Types of Exams

Unit Tests – Short tests conducted after every lesson or unit.
Mid-Term Exams – Conducted halfway through the term to evaluate performance.
Final Exams – Major exams at the end of the semester or academic year.
Online Practice Tests – Self-assessment quizzes to prepare for main exams.
Mock Tests – Simulated exams to help students understand exam patterns.
